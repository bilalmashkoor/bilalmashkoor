Hi, I’m Bilal — founder and builder behind [Tenxora](https://www.tenxora.com).

I’m building a more connected way for businesses to run: a platform for operational foundations, workflows, automation, integrations and AI that helps teams work with less friction.

**Work without friction.**

## What I’m building

- **Tenxora Platform** — a durable foundation for business data, processes, permissions and applications.
- **AI-assisted operations** — systems that can understand how a business works and help teams improve it.
- **Connected workflows** — fewer handoffs, repeated entries and disconnected tools.
- **Software people can adapt** — practical systems that grow with the operation instead of forcing the operation into a rigid box.


## How I think about building

The best software does not add another layer of complexity. It makes the work clearer, makes change safer and gives people more capacity to think.

I care about strong foundations, calm interfaces, useful automation and products that compound over time.
